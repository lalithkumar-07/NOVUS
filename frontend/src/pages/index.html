About the Hackathon

NOVUS Hackathon 2026 is a 24‑hour innovation challenge that brings together creative thinkers, developers, designers, and problem‑solvers from across institutions. Participants collaborate in teams to build impactful solutions for real‑world problems using technology and creativity.

With mentorship from industry experts, exciting challenges, and a competitive environment, NOVUS aims to spark innovation, encourage learning, and create a platform for students to showcase their skills. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced builder, this hackathon is your chance to experiment, network, and make something extraordinary
