Rules & Regulations

Hackathon Discipline & Conduct

  • • Participation is open to both individual participants and teams (maximum 4 members per team).
  • • A registration fee of ₹1700 per team is mandatory and is non‑refundable under any circumstances.
  • • All participants must be currently enrolled students of a recognized institution and must carry a valid College ID for verification.
  • • Each participant is required to bring their own laptop and necessary accessories (charger, extensions, adapters). The responsibility of maintaining the device lies entirely with the participant.
  • • Participants are solely responsible for the safety and security of their personal belongings, including laptops, mobile phones, and other valuable accessories. The organizers shall not be held liable for any loss or damage.
  • • Plagiarism, copying of code, or submission of pre‑built or previously published projects will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • • The decision of the judging panel shall be final and binding in all matters related to evaluation and results.
  • • All participants are hereby informed that strict discipline must be maintained throughout the hackathon without exception.
  • • Participants shall maintain silence and proper decorum inside the hackathon venue at all times.
  • • Disturbing or distracting other teams during working hours is strictly prohibited.
  • • Waste must be disposed of only in designated dustbins to ensure cleanliness of the venue.
  • • Equipment, systems, furniture, and all infrastructure provided must be handled with care and responsibility.
  • • Any damage to property shall be the sole responsibility of the concerned participant or team, and recovery charges will be applicable.
  • • Participants must strictly follow instructions issued by organizers, volunteers, mentors, and judges at all times.
  • • Any form of misbehavior, argument, or disrespect toward organizers, volunteers, mentors, or judges will result in immediate disciplinary action.
  • • Smoking, consumption of alcohol, or use of any prohibited substances within the premises is strictly banned.
  • • Unnecessary roaming inside or outside the designated hackathon areas is not permitted.
  • • Participants must be present during all scheduled sessions, reviews, checkpoints, and evaluations.
  • • Mobile phones must be kept in silent mode within the venue.
  • • Participants are informed that the selected food preference (Veg/Non‑Veg) during registration is final and no change requests will be entertained after submission, as catering arrangements are planned accordingly.
  • • Consumption of food and beverages is permitted only in designated areas.
  • • Participants are expected to maintain professional, respectful, and ethical behavior throughout the event.
  • • Entry to the venue is strictly limited to registered participants only.
  • • Personal belongings (bags, handbags, etc.) will be checked at the venue. Participants are requested to cooperate with the security process.
  • • Non‑compliance with any of the above rules will invite strict disciplinary action, including disqualification and removal from the venue.

Issued with approval of competent authority.
